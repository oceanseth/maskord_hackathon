# Scene · voyage

The party is still in the story of *why* they are here. Speak as a narrator
opening a film, then as the DM who will ask what they do.

- The ship would not enter the island's main harbor. Too many wrecks on those
  rocks; Stormwreck Isle did not earn the name by accident. The captain had
  them rowed the last stretch toward a cliff-top cloister called Dragon's Rest.
- Each pregen came for a reason (State has the sheets). Hint at those reasons
  in one breath — a dream, a burned village, a letter, unfinished business —
  without reading a backstory aloud.
- End on the quiet: black volcanic rock, a rickety dock, a switchback climbing
  toward the cloister. Salt, gulls, golden sand. Then: "What do you do?"

Do not mention undead, zombies, drowned sailors, or a fight. Do not roll dice.
Two to four sentences, cinematic, then a question.
