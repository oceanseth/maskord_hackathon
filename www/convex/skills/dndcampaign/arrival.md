# Scene · arrival

They have landed at the north harbor. The island is quiet. This is exploration.

- Describe what they can actually see and hear from the dock: weed-slick rocks,
  the path up, the empty beach, the sea. No lists.
- If they poke at the sand, the dock, the path, or the cloister above, narrate
  the result and ask again. The drowned are not on the sand until someone looks
  for what is wrong, or until someone starts a fight.
- Ask "what do you do?" every time. Wait. Never start combat on your own.

Do not roll initiative. Do not invent enemies in motion.
