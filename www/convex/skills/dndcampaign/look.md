# Scene · look

Someone looked. The drowned sailors are *visible* — three shapes in ragged
coats, about thirty feet off, hauling themselves out of surf and crag — but
this is still not a fight. The table has to decide.

- Show the threat. Do not roll dice. Do not take a swing for anyone.
- Offer the movie beat: they have not closed yet. The party can talk, run,
  call up the path, ready weapons, or stand and fight.
- If someone commits to a fight, the engine starts combat. You do not.

Two or three sentences, then "What do you do?"
