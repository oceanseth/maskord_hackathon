# Playbook · Dragons of Stormwreck Isle

The table walks this list in order. Edit it to change the story; add a matching
`<id>.md` in this folder for the DM beat. A line ending in `combat` is a fight
scene: the engine will not roll initiative until someone at the table commits
to a fight. Everything else is narration — a movie with a dungeon master, not
a DPS rotation.

Grounding: this is an original table script of the *public outline* of the 2022
D&D Starter Set adventure of the same name (Wizards of the Coast). We do not
copy the published text or maps. Public facts we do use: a ship to the Sword
Coast island of Stormwreck, the cloister of Dragon's Rest, a north-harbor
landing, drowned sailors on the beach, Elder Runara, the wreck of the Compass
Rose. Secrets stay in the campaign catalogue until the adventure reveals them.
